# Travel Insurance Guide

## Why travel insurance matters
Medical care abroad can be very expensive. Travel insurance protects you against medical emergencies,
trip cancellation, lost baggage and flight delays.

## What a good policy covers
- **Medical expenses and emergency evacuation:** look for at least USD 50,000 coverage for Asia trips.
- **Trip cancellation and interruption:** refunds for non-refundable bookings when you must cancel for covered reasons.
- **Baggage loss or delay:** compensation for lost items and essentials bought during a delay.
- **Flight delay:** a payout after a delay of a set number of hours (often 6 hours).
- **Personal liability:** damage or injury you accidentally cause to others.

## Common exclusions
- Pre-existing medical conditions (unless declared and accepted).
- Extreme sports unless you buy an add-on.
- Incidents under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

## Tips
- Buy insurance as soon as you book the trip so cancellation cover starts early.
- Save the insurer's 24-hour emergency number in your phone.
- Keep all receipts and reports (police report for theft, airline report for lost baggage).
