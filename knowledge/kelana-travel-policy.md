# KelanaAI Travel Partner Policy (Demo Company Document)

_This is a fictional company policy used to demonstrate that RAG answers come from private documents._

## Baggage allowance on KelanaAI partner packages
- **Backpacker packages:** 7 kg cabin baggage only; checked baggage can be added for USD 25 per 20 kg.
- **Standard packages:** 7 kg cabin baggage plus **20 kg** checked baggage.
- **Luxury packages:** 10 kg cabin baggage plus **30 kg** checked baggage and priority boarding.

## Cancellation and refunds
- Cancel more than 30 days before departure: full refund minus a USD 20 admin fee.
- Cancel 8-30 days before departure: 50% refund.
- Cancel 7 days or less before departure: no refund, but the trip can be rescheduled once for free.

## Payment
- A 30% deposit is required to confirm a booking; the balance is due 14 days before departure.
- Accepted methods: bank transfer, credit card and e-wallets (GoPay, OVO, DANA).

## Customer support
- 24/7 WhatsApp support for travellers on an active trip.
- Office hours: Monday-Friday, 09:00-17:00 WIB.
