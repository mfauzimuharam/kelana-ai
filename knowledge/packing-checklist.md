# Packing Checklist for International Trips

## Documents
- Passport (valid at least 6 months after arrival) and visa or visa waiver registration.
- Printed and digital copies of flight tickets, hotel bookings and travel insurance.
- Some local cash plus a debit/credit card that works abroad.

## Electronics
- Phone, charger and a power bank (power banks must go in **carry-on**, not checked baggage).
- Universal travel adapter. Japan uses Type A plugs (100V); Korea uses Type C/F (220V); Singapore uses Type G (230V).
- eSIM or pocket Wi-Fi for mobile data.

## Health
- Personal medication with prescription and doctor's letter.
- Basic first-aid kit, hand sanitiser and sunscreen.

## Clothing by season
- Summer (June-August in Japan/Korea): light, breathable clothes; it is hot and humid.
- Winter (December-February): thermal layers, a warm coat, gloves and a scarf.
- Comfortable walking shoes — expect 15,000+ steps per day in big cities.

## Family travel extras
- Snacks, a compact stroller and entertainment for long flights.
- Child-sized medication and copies of children's passports.
