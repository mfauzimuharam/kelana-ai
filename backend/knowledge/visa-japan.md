# Japan Visa Guide for Indonesian Travelers

_Last reviewed: 2026. Always confirm with the Embassy of Japan in Indonesia before travel._

## Who needs a visa for Japan
Indonesian passport holders generally need a visa to visit Japan for tourism.
There is an exception for holders of an Indonesian **electronic passport (e-passport)**:
after registering the e-passport for a **visa waiver** at the Embassy or Consulate of Japan,
you may stay in Japan for up to **15 days** for tourism, business meetings or visiting relatives.
Stays longer than 15 days require a regular temporary visitor visa.

## Visa waiver registration (e-passport)
- Apply at the Embassy of Japan in Jakarta or a Consulate General of Japan (Surabaya, Medan, Makassar, Denpasar).
- Bring your e-passport and the completed visa waiver registration form.
- Registration is valid for 3 years or until the passport expires, whichever is earlier.
- Each stay is limited to 15 days.

## Tourist visa requirements (single entry)
- Valid passport (at least 6 months validity recommended).
- Completed visa application form and a recent passport photo (4.5 cm x 3.5 cm).
- Flight reservation or itinerary (schedule of stay in Japan).
- Proof of funds, such as a recent bank statement (last 3 months).
- Certificate of employment or student ID, if applicable.
- Processing time is usually around 5 working days.

## Entering Japan
- Fill in the arrival information on **Visit Japan Web** before the flight to speed up immigration and customs.
- Keep your return or onward ticket and accommodation details ready.
- Immigration takes fingerprints and a photo on arrival.
