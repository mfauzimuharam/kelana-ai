# Japan Travel Guide

## Top attractions in Tokyo
- **Senso-ji Temple (Asakusa):** Tokyo's oldest temple; walk Nakamise Street for snacks and souvenirs.
- **Shibuya Crossing:** the world's busiest pedestrian crossing; view it from Shibuya Sky.
- **Meiji Shrine and Harajuku:** a peaceful forest shrine next to the colourful Takeshita Street.
- **Tokyo Tower and Tokyo Skytree:** city views; Skytree is the tallest tower in Japan.
- **Akihabara:** electronics, anime and gaming district.
- **teamLab Planets / Borderless:** immersive digital art museums, popular with families.

## Day trips from Tokyo
- **Mount Fuji and Lake Kawaguchiko:** about 2 hours by highway bus; clearest views in winter mornings.
- **Hakone:** hot springs (onsen), Lake Ashi cruise and views of Mount Fuji.
- **Nikko:** UNESCO-listed shrines and waterfalls.
- **Kamakura:** the Great Buddha and seaside temples.

## Kyoto and Osaka
- Kyoto: Fushimi Inari Shrine, Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion), Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, Gion district.
- Osaka: Dotonbori street food, Osaka Castle, Universal Studios Japan.
- Tokyo to Kyoto takes about 2 hours 15 minutes by Shinkansen (bullet train).

## Getting around Japan
- Use an IC card (Suica, PASMO or ICOCA) for trains, buses and convenience stores.
- The Japan Rail Pass is mainly worth it for long-distance Shinkansen trips across several cities.
- Trains are punctual; avoid rush hour (7:30-9:30 and 17:30-19:30) with large luggage.

## Food to try
- Sushi and kaisen-don at Tsukiji Outer Market.
- Ramen, takoyaki and okonomiyaki (Osaka specialities).
- Halal food is available in Tokyo and Osaka; look for halal-certified restaurants and prayer rooms at major stations and airports.

## Money and costs
- Currency: Japanese Yen (JPY). Cash is still common at small shops and temples.
- Budget travellers can spend roughly USD 70-100 per day; mid-range USD 150-250 per day.
- Tipping is not customary in Japan.

## Etiquette
- Be quiet on trains and avoid phone calls.
- Remove shoes when entering homes, ryokan and some temples.
- Carry your rubbish with you; public bins are rare.
