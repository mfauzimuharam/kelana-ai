# Bringing Medication into Japan

_Always confirm with the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) of Japan before travel._

## Personal-use medication
- You may bring medication for personal use, generally up to a **one-month supply** of prescription medicine
  and up to a two-month supply of most over-the-counter medicine, without prior approval.
- Bring a copy of your **prescription** and a **letter from your doctor** (in English) describing the medicine and dosage.
- Keep medicine in its original packaging.

## When you need prior approval (Yunyu Kakunin-sho)
- If you bring more than the permitted quantity, you must apply in advance for an import confirmation
  certificate called **Yunyu Kakunin-sho** from MHLW.
- Certain controlled substances (for example some narcotics and psychotropic medicines) need a special permit.

## Prohibited medicines
- Stimulants such as amphetamine-based ADHD medication (e.g. Adderall) are **prohibited** in Japan, even with a prescription.
- Some cold and allergy medicines containing **pseudoephedrine** above a small concentration are prohibited.

## Medical devices
- Syringes, CPAP machines and similar personal medical devices are allowed for personal use; carry a doctor's letter.
