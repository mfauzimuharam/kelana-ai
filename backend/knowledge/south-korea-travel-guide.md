# South Korea Travel Guide

_Always confirm entry rules with the Embassy of the Republic of Korea before travel._

## Visa for Indonesian travelers
- Indonesian passport holders need a **visa** to visit South Korea for tourism (Indonesia is not on the K-ETA visa-free list).
- Typical documents: passport, application form, photo, bank statement, employment letter and flight itinerary.
- **Jeju Island** allows visa-free entry for many nationalities, including Indonesia, for up to 30 days when flying directly to Jeju.

## Top attractions in Seoul
- **Gyeongbokgung Palace:** wear a hanbok for free entry; watch the changing of the guard.
- **Bukchon Hanok Village:** traditional houses between two palaces.
- **Myeongdong:** shopping and street food.
- **N Seoul Tower:** city views from Namsan Mountain.
- **Hongdae:** youth culture, live music and cafés.

## Beyond Seoul
- **Busan:** Haeundae Beach, Gamcheon Culture Village and Jagalchi Fish Market (about 2.5 hours by KTX train).
- **Jeju Island:** volcanic landscapes, Hallasan Mountain and Seongsan Ilchulbong.

## Getting around
- Use a **T-money** card for subways and buses.
- KTX high-speed trains connect Seoul with Busan, Daegu and Gyeongju.

## Food
- Korean BBQ, bibimbap, tteokbokki and fried chicken.
- Muslim travellers can find halal restaurants around Itaewon near the Seoul Central Mosque.

## Seasons
- Spring (April) for cherry blossoms; autumn (October-November) for foliage; winter is cold (below 0°C).
