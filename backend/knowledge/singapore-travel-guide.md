# Singapore Travel Guide

## Visa for Indonesian travelers
- Indonesian passport holders can visit Singapore **visa-free for up to 30 days** for tourism (ASEAN visa exemption).
- Passport must be valid for at least **6 months**.
- All travellers must submit the **SG Arrival Card** (free, online via the ICA website or MyICA app) within 3 days before arrival.

## Top attractions
- **Marina Bay Sands and the Merlion:** iconic skyline; free light show "Spectra" in the evening.
- **Gardens by the Bay:** Supertree Grove (free) and the Cloud Forest and Flower Dome (paid).
- **Sentosa Island:** Universal Studios Singapore, S.E.A. Aquarium and beaches.
- **Chinatown, Little India and Kampong Glam:** heritage districts; Masjid Sultan is in Kampong Glam.
- **Singapore Zoo and Night Safari:** great for families.

## Getting around
- The MRT is clean and efficient; pay with a contactless bank card or an EZ-Link card.
- Grab is the main ride-hailing app.

## Food
- Hawker centres such as Lau Pa Sat, Maxwell Food Centre and Newton serve affordable local food.
- Try chicken rice, laksa, chilli crab and satay. Many stalls are halal-certified by MUIS.

## Rules to know
- Chewing gum sales are banned; littering and smoking in non-designated areas carry heavy fines.
- No eating or drinking on the MRT.
